Sky Timelapse

Sky Timelapse is a personal, self-hosted tool that records the sky from a single home camera, edits the footage into a weekly timelapse, and uploads it to its owner's own YouTube channel (HowToTech Portal).

It is used only by its owner. It does not offer sign-in to anyone else and does not access any other YouTube account.
